# INC-1099: CloudWatch alarms silently unrouted — SNS subscription deleted

## Summary
The PagerDuty HTTPS subscription on `acme-prod-alarms` (SNS) was deleted during a
PagerDuty integration key rotation (the old subscription was removed before the new one was
confirmed). For 3 days, CloudWatch alarms fired to a topic with no subscribers; nothing
paged. Discovered when an RDS storage alarm was noticed in the console.

## Impact
No missed SEV-1/2 (Prometheus-based paging was unaffected), but 4 CloudWatch alarms went
unacknowledged, including `FreeStorageSpace` on `prod-reporting-pg`.

## Root cause
Manual subscription management outside Terraform; no dead-man's switch.

## Remediation
Subscription recreated via Terraform.

## Prevention
Heartbeat alarm (`acme-prod-alarm-heartbeat`) fires every hour and must be acknowledged by
PagerDuty automation; `sns:Unsubscribe` alerts in CloudTrail; subscriptions Terraform-only.
