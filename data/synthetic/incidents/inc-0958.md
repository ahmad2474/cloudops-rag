# INC-0958: Payments failed for 6 minutes during Stripe API key rotation

## Summary
Manual rotation of the Stripe secret key: the engineer revoked the **old** key in the Stripe
dashboard immediately after `put-secret-value`, before ESO refresh and pod restart. All
Stripe calls returned `401` for 6 minutes.

## Impact
100% of card payments failed for 6 min (~900 attempts; ~85% retried).

## Root cause
Runbook order not followed: revoke old key only after consumers have rolled.

## Remediation
Force-sync ESO, `rollout restart`; recovered.

## Prevention
- `secret-rotation` runbook: explicit "revoke last" step with a 15-minute wait and a
  verification query (`stripe_requests_total{status="401"} == 0`).
- Rotation of third-party keys requires a second engineer on a call.
