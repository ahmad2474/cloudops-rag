# INC-0948: Kyverno audit found 14 prod workloads without resource limits

## Summary
Enabling `require-requests-limits` in **audit** mode surfaced 14 Deployments/CronJobs with
no memory limits, including two `data-pipeline` CronJobs that had caused node memory
pressure twice in May.

## Impact
None directly; recorded as SEV-4 to track remediation.

## Remediation
All 14 fixed by owning teams within 3 days; policy switched to **enforce** on 2025-06-23.

## Prevention
Policies are introduced audit → enforce with a two-week window; report posted in `#platform`.
