# INC-0994: CoreDNS OOM from ndots amplification after payments SDK upgrade

## Summary
The PSP SDK upgrade in `payments-api` disabled its internal DNS cache. With `ndots:5`,
each external call generated 5–6 lookups; CoreDNS QPS rose 9× and two replicas were
OOMKilled (170 Mi limit), causing SERVFAILs.

## Impact
0.8% of payment requests saw DNS errors for 50 min (retried by the SDK).

## Root cause
DNS amplification from the search path plus loss of client-side caching.

## Remediation
Scaled CoreDNS 4 → 8; re-enabled SDK cache.

## Prevention
`ndots:2` for `payments-api`; CoreDNS `cache` TTL 30 s; autoscaler QPS-aware (custom
metric) instead of node-count-only.
