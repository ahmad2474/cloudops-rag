# INC-0952: payments-api outage — `prod-payments-pg` connection exhaustion

## Summary
A merchant campaign drove traffic +180%. HPA scaled `payments-api` to 30 replicas × 32-connection
pools, exceeding `max_connections = 1000`. Postgres refused new connections
(`FATAL: remaining connection slots are reserved`); readiness probes that depended on the
pool failed and the ALB returned 503.

## Impact
38% of payment requests failed for 41 min, degraded for 33 more. ~1,500 attempts not retried.
Monthly availability budget: 71% consumed.

## Symptoms
- `DatabaseConnections` flat at 1000
- app: `FATAL: remaining connection slots are reserved for non-replication superuser connections`
- ALB 503; target group healthy count collapsing

## Investigation
CPU on the instance was 34% — not the bottleneck. `pg_stat_activity` grouped by
`application_name` showed `payments-api` at 960.

## Root cause
No relationship between replica count and the database connection budget.

## Remediation
1. HPA max 40 → 20; 2. pool size 32 → 16; 3. RDS Proxy adopted 2025-07-03.

## Prevention
See `postmortem-inc-0952`: RDS Proxy, per-role `CONNECTION LIMIT`, chart CI check,
readiness decoupled from DB, alarms page owning team.
