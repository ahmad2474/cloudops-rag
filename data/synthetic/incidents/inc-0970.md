# INC-0970: `prod-identity-pg` storage full — WAL retained by an abandoned replication slot

## Summary
A logical replication slot created for a cancelled analytics experiment (`debezium_identity`)
was never dropped. WAL accumulated for 6 weeks (`TransactionLogsDiskUsage` 410 GB) until
storage autoscaling hit its 500 GB ceiling; the instance went `storage-full` and read-only.

## Impact
Logins that write (`last_login`) failed 85 min; read-only token validation continued.

## Timeline (UTC)
- 03:15 `FreeStorageSpace < 15%` alarm — then 0 within 10 min (WAL growth was steep).
- 03:30 `SELECT * FROM pg_replication_slots` → inactive slot, `restart_lsn` 6 weeks old.
- 03:35 `pg_drop_replication_slot('debezium_identity')`; WAL recycled over the next hour.
- 04:40 Storage 55% free; instance writable; resolved.

## Root cause
Orphaned replication slot; no alarm on `OldestReplicationSlotLag`.

## Prevention
Alarms on `OldestReplicationSlotLag > 10 GB` and `TransactionLogsDiskUsage > 50 GB`;
slots are created only via Terraform with an owner tag; weekly inactive-slot report.
