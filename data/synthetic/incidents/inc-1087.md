# INC-1087: Duplicate emails — SQS visibility timeout shorter than processing time

## Summary
A template-rendering change made email jobs take 40–70 s; the queue's visibility timeout
was 30 s. Messages became visible again mid-processing and were consumed by another worker.
~22k customers received 2–4 copies of a statement email.

## Impact
Customer confusion; 600 support contacts; apology email.

## Root cause
No idempotency key on send; visibility timeout not tied to processing SLA.

## Remediation
Workers paused; visibility timeout 30 → 300 s; backlog resumed.

## Prevention
Idempotency key (`message_id`) stored in DynamoDB with a 24 h TTL before send; alarm on
`ApproximateNumberOfMessagesNotVisible` re-deliveries (`ApproximateReceiveCount > 1`).
