# INC-1075: Prometheus remote-write backlog delayed alerts by 12 minutes

## Summary
Prometheus in `prod-use1-a` fell behind on remote-write to Mimir (WAL segments 3.2 GB
queued) after a Mimir distributor rollout. Alerts evaluated in Mimir (the recording-rule
based SLO alerts) fired 12 min late; local Prometheus alerts were on time.

## Impact
Delayed SLO burn alert for an unrelated `ledger-service` blip.

## Root cause
Remote-write queue shards under-configured; distributor restart caused a burst.

## Remediation
`queue_config.max_shards` 50 → 200; backlog drained in 20 min.

## Prevention
Critical alerts (pages) evaluated locally in Prometheus; Mimir used for dashboards and
warnings only. Alarm on `prometheus_remote_storage_highest_timestamp_in_seconds - … > 120`.
