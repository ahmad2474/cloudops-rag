# INC-0977: DNS timeouts from conntrack exhaustion on high-traffic nodes

## Summary
Nodes hosting `payments-api` reached `nf_conntrack_max` (131072). New UDP DNS flows
were dropped; ~3% of requests hit a 5 s DNS timeout, breaching the p99 SLO for 110 min.

## Investigation
`node_nf_conntrack_entries / node_nf_conntrack_entries_limit` = 0.99 on 6 nodes. Most
entries were UDP 53 to CoreDNS ClusterIP with 30 s timeouts, driven by `ndots:5` lookups
from the payments SDK (5 queries per external call).

## Root cause
UDP DNS churn through the ClusterIP conntrack path on busy nodes.

## Remediation
Raised `nf_conntrack_max` to 524288 via the node bootstrap; restarted affected pods.

## Prevention
NodeLocal DNSCache deployed (2025-08-20) — node-local cache, TCP upstream, no conntrack for
pod→cache; `ndots:2` for external-heavy services. Alarm at 80% of conntrack limit.
