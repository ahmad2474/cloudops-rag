# INC-0941: notification-worker auth failures after Secrets Manager rotation

## Summary
Scheduled rotation of `acme/prod/rds/prod-identity-pg/app` succeeded at 06:00. ESO
updated the Kubernetes Secret at 06:18 (refresh interval 1 h). `notification-worker`
reads credentials once at startup and had no Reloader annotation, so it kept the old
password until restarted.

## Impact
Email/SMS notifications delayed ~75 min.

## Root cause
Consumer does not tolerate rotation (no pool refresh, no restart trigger).

## Remediation
`kubectl rollout restart deploy/notification-worker -n notifications`.

## Prevention
Reloader annotation required by `kubernetes-workload-standards` §9; audit found 5 other
services in the same state — all fixed by 2025-06-10.
