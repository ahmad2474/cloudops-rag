# INC-0963: webhook-dispatcher retry storm against a merchant endpoint

## Summary
A large merchant's webhook receiver began returning 500. `webhook-dispatcher` retried with
a fixed 1 s interval and no cap per destination; 30 k events × retries produced ~2,000 req/s
to one host, saturating NAT port allocation for 1a and delaying webhooks for **all** merchants.

## Impact
Webhook delivery p95 12 min for 85 min; one merchant's endpoint effectively DoS'd by us
(apology sent).

## Investigation
NAT `ErrorPortAllocation` in 1a; `webhook-dispatcher` outbound connections to a single IP
in `TIME_WAIT` exhaustion. A security-group egress rule was added in the console to block
the destination as a stopgap (later found as drift — INC-0973).

## Root cause
No exponential backoff, no per-destination concurrency limit, no circuit breaker.

## Remediation
Blocked destination; drained queue after the merchant fixed their side.

## Prevention
Backoff (1 s → 1 h, jitter), per-destination concurrency 8, circuit breaker (open after 50
consecutive failures, half-open probe every 5 min). Shipped 2025-07-22.
