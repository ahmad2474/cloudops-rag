# INC-1093: ExternalDNS deleted public records after TXT ownership prefix change

## Summary
A chart upgrade changed ExternalDNS's `txtPrefix`. The new instance did not recognise the
existing ownership TXT records, treated the A/ALIAS records as foreign, and — with
`policy: sync` — **deleted** `checkout.acmepay.com` in Route 53 before recreating it with
the new prefix. Cached resolvers masked most of it; ~5% of new visitors got NXDOMAIN for 30 min.

## Impact
Checkout unreachable for a subset of users 30 min.

## Root cause
Ownership identity change under `sync` policy.

## Remediation
Records recreated automatically after the new TXT was written; TTL wait.

## Prevention
ExternalDNS runs with `policy: upsert-only` in prod; ownership-prefix changes require a
migration procedure (both prefixes registered).
