# INC-0909: ledger read replica lag 20 minutes during autovacuum

## Summary
`prod-ledger-pg-ro-1` fell 20 min behind the primary while an aggressive autovacuum on
`ledger_entries` (1.1 B rows) ran concurrently with the nightly export. `reporting-api`
served stale balances.

## Impact
Merchant dashboard balances stale for up to 20 min between 02:40 and 04:05 UTC. Two support
tickets.

## Timeline (UTC)
- 02:10 Autovacuum starts on `ledger_entries` (wraparound prevention).
- 02:30 Nightly export job starts (reads from replica, heavy sequential scans).
- 02:40 `ReplicaLag > 60s` alarm; `dbre-oncall` paged.
- 03:10 Export paused; lag recovers slowly (replica IOPS saturated at 12k).
- 04:05 Lag < 5 s; export resumed at lower parallelism.

## Root cause
Replica IOPS (gp3 default 12k) saturated by vacuum replay + export scans. `max_standby_streaming_delay`
caused replay to wait on long-running export queries.

## Remediation
Replica storage IOPS 12k → 20k; export parallelism 8 → 4.

## Prevention
- `autovacuum_vacuum_cost_limit` tuned for `ledger_entries`; scheduled manual vacuum on Sundays 05:00.
- Export window moved to 04:00 after autovacuum typically completes.
- `hot_standby_feedback = on` on the replica.
