# INC-1040: Grafana dashboards empty — Mimir ingesters out of disk

## Summary
Mimir ingesters in `acme-shared` filled their 200 GB volumes after a cardinality spike
(a new `payments-api` metric with `merchant_id` label — 4k series × 20 histogram
buckets). Remote-write from prod Prometheus was rejected (`429`) and dashboards for the
last 2 h were blank; alerting from Prometheus itself (local) continued.

## Impact
Visibility gap 1 h 45; no alerts lost.

## Root cause
Unbounded label cardinality; no per-tenant series limit.

## Remediation
Metric relabelled (drop `merchant_id`); ingester volumes expanded to 400 GB.

## Prevention
Mimir per-tenant `max_global_series_per_user`; CI check rejects metrics with
high-cardinality labels (`merchant_id`, `user_id`, `request_id`).
