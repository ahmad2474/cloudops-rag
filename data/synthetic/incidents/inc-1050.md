# INC-1050: SMS notifications delayed — vendor incident

## Summary
The SMS vendor reported elevated delivery latency in us-east-1. `notification-worker`
queued messages; delivery p95 reached 25 min. Email channel unaffected.

## Impact
One-time passcodes by SMS delayed; login via email OTP continued. 340 support contacts.

## Root cause
Third-party outage.

## Remediation
Enabled the secondary SMS provider via feature flag at 16:10 (should have been 15:30).

## Prevention
Automatic failover to the secondary provider when vendor p95 > 2 min for 5 min; vendor
status page polled into the alerting pipeline.
