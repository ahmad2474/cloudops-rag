# INC-1084: Month-end deploy freeze bypassed by a hotfix

## Summary
A `reporting-api` hotfix was synced in prod during the month-end freeze (last 3 business
days). The engineer used `argocd app sync --force` from a Gatekeeper session; the freeze is
enforced only in the UI via the `incident` label. No impact, but the ledger close depends
on `reporting-api` stability.

## Root cause
Freeze enforced by convention/UI, not by policy.

## Prevention
ArgoCD sync windows (`deny` windows for prod apps during freeze); `--force` requires IC
approval recorded in the change ticket.
