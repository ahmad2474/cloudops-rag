# INC-0934: HPA stopped scaling — metrics-server certificate expired

## Summary
`kubectl get hpa -A` showed `<unknown>/70%` for every HPA. metrics-server's serving
certificate (self-signed, 1-year, from the 2024 Helm install) had expired; the API aggregation
layer rejected it. `payments-api` stayed at 12 replicas through a traffic ramp.

## Impact
p99 for `payments-api` at 380 ms (limit 400) for 30 min; no errors.

## Root cause
metrics-server deployed with `--kubelet-insecure-tls` and a static cert instead of the
EKS-managed add-on.

## Remediation
Replaced with the EKS `metrics-server` add-on (cert rotated automatically).

## Prevention
Alarm on `apiserver_aggregator_unavailable_apiservice > 0`; HPA `<unknown>` metric alarm.
