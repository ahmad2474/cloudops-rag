# INC-1078: ALB 504s — payments-api waited indefinitely on a slow PSP

## Summary
The card PSP's authorisation endpoint slowed to 40–90 s. `payments-api` had a 120 s HTTP
client timeout; the ALB idle timeout is 60 s, so clients received 504 while the pod kept the
request (and a DB connection) open. Worker pools filled; even non-PSP endpoints slowed.

## Impact
Card authorisations 30% failure (504) for 55 min; bank transfers unaffected once pools freed.

## Root cause
Downstream timeout longer than the edge timeout; no bulkhead per dependency.

## Remediation
PSP client timeout 120 → 8 s with 1 retry; separate worker pool for PSP calls.

## Prevention
Standard: all outbound timeouts < 30 s and < ALB idle timeout; per-dependency bulkheads;
circuit breaker (like INC-0963's webhook fix) generalised into the shared HTTP client.
