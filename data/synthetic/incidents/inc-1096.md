# INC-1096: Spot interruption wave in `us-east-1a` plus strict PDBs

## Summary
AWS reclaimed 11 of 14 spot nodes in 1a within 4 minutes. Karpenter's on-demand fallback
launched replacements, but `ledger-service`'s PDB (`maxUnavailable: 1`) plus zone
spread constraints (`maxSkew: 1`, `DoNotSchedule`) left 8 pods Pending for 40 min while
1a capacity recovered.

## Impact
Ledger p99 > 800 ms for 45 min; webhooks delayed 20 min.

## Root cause
Spread constraint too strict for a single-AZ capacity loss; spot share in 1a too high (no
per-AZ cap).

## Remediation
Temporarily `whenUnsatisfiable: ScheduleAnyway`; Karpenter provisioned OD in 1b/1c.

## Prevention
NodePool `capacity-type` requirement mixes spot/OD per AZ (max 60% spot per zone);
spread constraints use `ScheduleAnyway` with `minDomains`; interruption-rate alarm.
