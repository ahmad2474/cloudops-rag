# INC-1007: `risk` namespace deploys rejected after the EKS 1.31 upgrade

## Summary
After the 1.29 → 1.31 control-plane upgrade, every pod create in `risk` was denied by the
Kyverno policy `risk-legacy-psp-bridge` (`unknown field` under strict validation).
`fraud-scoring` could not be rescheduled; it ran at 2/3 replicas after a node replacement.

## Impact
Scoring latency +15% for ~2 h; no failed payments.

## Root cause
A 2024 policy mirroring PodSecurityPolicy semantics used a field path that 1.31 validates
strictly. `risk` was excluded from the staging soak (no `memory` pool in staging).

## Remediation
Policy deleted; standard Kyverno set already covers the intent.

## Prevention
See `postmortem-inc-1007`: bridge-policy inventory, staging `memory` pool, `kyverno test`
in upgrade pre-flight.
