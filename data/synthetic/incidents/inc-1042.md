# INC-1042: Pods Pending with idle capacity

## Summary
During a merchant webhook storm, 22 pods stayed `Pending` although cluster CPU averaged
31% and node count was normal. Karpenter had consolidated workloads onto `m6i.large` nodes
whose `EC2NodeClass` lacked `kubelet.maxPods`; those nodes advertised 29 allocatable pods
(non-prefix-delegation default) and ipamd could not hand out more IPs.

## Impact
Webhook delivery p95 up to 6 min for ~90 min; 0 lost.

## Symptoms
- `Too many pods` and `failed to assign an IP address to pod` events
- Subnet free IPs > 40k (**not** subnet exhaustion — unlike INC-0981)
- `kubectl get node -o jsonpath='{.status.allocatable.pods}'` → 29 on Karpenter nodes vs 110 on MNG

## Root cause
Karpenter max-pods calculation did not account for prefix delegation; consolidation
concentrated pods on the affected nodes.

## Remediation
Cordoned affected nodes; NodePool minimum size `xlarge`; `maxPods: 110` in the NodeClass.

## Prevention
Explicit `maxPods` in every NodeClass with CI check; `consolidateAfter: 5m` + peak
disruption budget; runbook §B; allocatable-pods alarm. Details: `postmortem-inc-1042`.
