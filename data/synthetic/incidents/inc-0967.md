# INC-0967: CloudWatch Logs ingestion throttled — Fluent Bit backpressure

## Summary
A debug-level logging change in `ledger-service` multiplied log volume 40×. CloudWatch
`PutLogEvents` throttled (`ThrottlingException`), Fluent Bit buffers filled, and logs for
**every** namespace on affected nodes were delayed up to 2 h.

## Impact
Observability gap; an unrelated `identity-service` investigation was slowed. No customer impact.

## Root cause
Per-account `PutLogEvents` TPS quota reached; Fluent Bit shared one output pipeline for all
namespaces with a small filesystem buffer.

## Remediation
Reverted the logging level; requested quota increase (granted).

## Prevention
- Fluent Bit: per-namespace outputs, `storage.type filesystem` with 2 GB limit, and a
  drop-oldest policy for non-critical namespaces.
- Alarm on `IncomingBytes` per log group > 3× 7-day baseline.
- Log level changes to prod require a PR label `log-volume-reviewed`.
