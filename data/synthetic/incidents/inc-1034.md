# INC-1034: data-pipeline Spark drivers on `general` nodes evicting neighbours

## Summary
A Spark job's chart lost its `workload=memory` toleration in a refactor. Drivers (24 Gi
each) landed on `general` m6i.xlarge nodes, triggered `MemoryPressure`, and kubelet evicted
BestEffort/Burstable neighbours — mostly `notification-worker`.

## Impact
Notification delays ~50 min; 3 nodes under pressure.

## Root cause
Missing toleration/selector; no memory limit on the driver.

## Remediation
Job killed; chart fixed.

## Prevention
Standard §13 check in chart CI (memory > 8 Gi ⇒ must target the `memory` pool);
`LimitRange` in `data` namespace caps default memory at 8 Gi.
