# INC-1066: `verify-images` policy blocked all prod deploys

## Summary
Kyverno `verify-images` (cosign signature required) was switched to enforce in prod. Images
built before 2026-05-01 were unsigned; any pod restart or HPA scale-out for those services
was rejected (`image not verified`). A node replacement at 09:40 left `identity-service`
at 2/4 replicas.

## Impact
Reduced redundancy for 4 services ~2 h; identity p99 +20%; no errors.

## Root cause
Policy enforced without back-signing running images.

## Remediation
Policy to audit; all currently deployed digests signed by a one-off job; re-enforced 05-20.

## Prevention
Enforce rollout requires a report of unsigned running images = 0; `cosign sign` step
back-fills in the pipeline for hotfix branches.
