# INC-1090: Nodes NotReady from disk pressure — container log growth

## Summary
`fraud-scoring` began logging a full feature vector per request (debug left enabled).
Container logs on two `memory` nodes grew to 60 GB in 3 h (root volume 80 GB); kubelet
reported `DiskPressure`, evicted pods, and went `NotReady`.

## Impact
Scoring at reduced capacity 70 min; no failed payments (fail-open to rules).

## Root cause
No per-container log rotation limits; debug logging in prod.

## Remediation
Nodes replaced; log level reverted.

## Prevention
containerd `max_container_log_line_size` and kubelet `containerLogMaxSize: 50Mi`,
`containerLogMaxFiles: 3` in the NodeClass; log-volume alarm per namespace (from INC-0967).
