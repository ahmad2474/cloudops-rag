# INC-0988: IRSA broken in the DR cluster after OIDC provider recreation

## Summary
`prod-usw2-a` was rebuilt during a DR exercise. Its OIDC issuer URL changed; IRSA role
trust policies that had been hand-edited (not Terraform-managed) still referenced the old
issuer ID. Every AWS call from pods failed with
`not authorized to perform: sts:AssumeRoleWithWebIdentity`.

## Impact
DR cluster non-functional for 85 min (no customer traffic on it).

## Root cause
Trust policies diverged from Terraform (two roles edited manually in 2025-05).

## Remediation
`atlantis apply` on the IRSA stack restored trust policies from the new cluster's OIDC output.

## Prevention
Drift detection extended to IAM trust policies; Config rule flags roles whose trust references
an OIDC provider that no longer exists.
